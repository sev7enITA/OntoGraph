    How other platforms use weights

    Neo4j uses relationship costs to calculate weighted paths. ServiceNow models service impact. NVIDIA Auto Ontology compares data values to score potential foreign keys. Palantir supports properties on links and reviewed ontology changes. OntoBricks supports sign-off quorums. OnToGraPh brings consequence, confidence, source and reviewer decisions together in a portable release. A team can require evidence for every dependency of a critical process and independently verify what each consuming application did after a change.

    The adoption test is whether a steward can identify a consequential, uncertain dependency, recover the source and route the change to the right owner more reliably than with the existing workflow. Comparative performance and customer savings still require a pilot.

    When assessors disagree

    Finance and PMO can record independent positions, grades, dates and sources on one mapping. A designated reviewer selects a supported assertion, gives a reason and approves that candidate. Changing the claims requires a new review.
