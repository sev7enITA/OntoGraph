# Who already uses weighted relationships?

**Market and practitioner review · 25 November 2026**

Weighted graphs are established technology. Neo4j computes weighted routes, ServiceNow propagates operational impact through a service topology, and Palantir can govern edits to an ontology whose links carry properties. A buyer evaluating OnToGraPh should compare the work of tracing one business decision from its supporting relationship assessment through review, release, consumer observation and eventual withdrawal. A team that already does this well has a credible incumbent.

## What practitioners actually reported

This is a targeted qualitative sample of four first-person reports selected for concrete examples of interpretation, missing evidence and changed dependencies. It includes issue trackers and a vendor-hosted community, with dates checked against the original posts. It samples people who encountered friction. Counts here give neither market prevalence nor buying intent. The design implications are our interpretation.

| Signal | Implication for the design |
| --- | --- |
| **4 December 2024 · Neo4j Community.** A user optimized a route by comfort cost and then tried to inspect its physical distance. The returned virtual relationships exposed cost, so the user could not recover the distinct quantity as expected. [Question and maintainer response](https://community.neo4j.com/t/problem-with-relationships-path-of-dijkstra-algorithm/71754) | Keep the original source, predicate, target and assessment visible for each route step. Label the scale and aggregation so the reader can reconstruct the summary. |
| **29 April 2024 · OpenCTI.** An implementer reported a fixed low confidence on imported sightings despite supplying an input value. The issue was closed in January 2025. [Historical report and fix discussion](https://github.com/OpenCTI-Platform/opencti/issues/6835) | Distinguish absent assessments from assessed zero. Preserve numbers during export and import and test both conditions. |
| **3 October 2025 · OpenCTI.** An operator could edit a lower-confidence entity yet could not delete it when an attached relationship carried a higher confidence. The operator asked whether the authorization consequence was intended. [Operator report](https://github.com/OpenCTI-Platform/opencti/issues/12633) | Keep an assessment from implicitly granting or revoking authority. A human with the proper role approves or withdraws the release through explicit operations. |
| **4 November 2026 · DataHub.** A user reported that removing a dbt model's last dependency left the earlier upstream edge in the stored lineage. They compared the manifest with the backend graph API. This is a report against the identified version. [Reproduction and observed output](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/issues/20951) | Compare released models and independently check the affected consumer after a change. A source declaration and a consumer observation answer different questions. |

## Decision test for a buyer

A Finance export and a PMO file can use the same WBS code for different objects. Put a deliberately uncertain mapping on a high-consequence path. In a second arm, represent the same case with the incumbent. Ask two reviewers to identify the affected control, recover the source and rationale for each assessed edge, locate missing assessments, and decide what evidence they need before publication. Include a consumer that reports the new release but still gives an old-rule answer.

Measure time to recover the decision evidence, high-consequence cases missed, missing assessments incorrectly treated as zero, and consumer outcomes independently checked. Record setup and ongoing maintenance effort for both arms. The commercial hypothesis is that a portable semantic release with a bounded impact explanation and per-consumer checks can reduce this work for teams operating across silos. The synthetic example establishes how to run the comparison. A customer pilot establishes its value.

## Independent model challenge

Gemini 4 Neon High used search-grounded summaries to scout competing implementations and challenge the proposed design. We independently inspected the cited primary documents and tagged code. Its finding about OntoBricks review quorums is reflected in the comparison. We accepted its objections to multiplying ordinal severity by confidence, adding the same exposure across reconverging paths, and visually concealing unassessed links. A path explanation should retain access to each step's original assessment. Its suggested fixed confidence caps for different evidence classes would require calibration with observed outcomes before they could be defended. No model-generated product absence or unverified issue number was used as market evidence.

## Evidence limits

The comparison covers documented workflows and pinned implementations where available. The research included no deployments or head-to-head vendor benchmarks. A customer can build equivalent workflows on an incumbent. Confidence is a steward's judgment on the asserted relationship. Criticality is an ordinal consequence assessment. Neither supplies calibrated loss probabilities, predicted financial loss or an automatic approval.
